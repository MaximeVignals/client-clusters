Client Clusters

Plan your client rounds

Import your client list (Excel or CSV with addresses, or a KMZ/KML from Google Earth or My Maps). Clients are placed on the map and grouped with their nearest neighbours, so each group can be visited in one trip. Then note each visit as you go.

Your file needs at least a client name and an address column (postal code and city can be separate columns). A “last visit” date column is picked up too. KMZ pins keep their exact position.
